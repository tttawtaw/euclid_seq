Euclid Seq - macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon)

1. Close your DAW.
2. Open Terminal, go to this folder (type "cd " then drag the folder onto
   the Terminal window and press Enter), and paste:

   mkdir -p ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   rm -rf ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/euclid_seq.clap ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/euclid_seq.vst3
   cp -R euclid_seq.clap ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/
   cp -R euclid_seq.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/euclid_seq.clap ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/euclid_seq.vst3

   The last line stops macOS from blocking the plugin because it was
   downloaded and isn't signed by a registered developer.

3. Open your DAW:
   - Bitwig: Euclid Seq (CLAP) appears in the browser. Put it before the instrument.
   - Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > turn on "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders"
     and Rescan. Put Euclid Seq on its own MIDI track, then set the instrument
     track's "MIDI From" to that track.
